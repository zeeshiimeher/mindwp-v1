# MindWP rationale

Why the current direction was chosen, written July 2026.

This is reasoning, not instruction. It never overrides [FOUNDATION.md](./FOUNDATION.md), [STRATEGY.md](./STRATEGY.md) or [WRITING.md](./WRITING.md). Read it when questioning the strategy. Skip it when doing the work.

## The situation

MindWP has two real capability areas: website work, and connected enquiry work.

Website work is the strongest current delivery skill, sells in a category buyers already understand, and proves itself through the finished result.

Connected enquiry work has better long-term economics but little published proof so far.

The question was: what should MindWP be known for, while keeping both independently sellable?

## Options considered

**Website-led.** Lead with premium websites; keep connected work as a strong second family. Easiest to sell now, easiest to prove. Risk: the business gets labelled a web agency and the wider ambition never arrives.

**Connected-enquiry-led.** Lead with enquiry handling. Best recurring economics. Rejected as a starting position: buyers say they want more customers, not better handling; the marketing evidence in that category is largely old or untraceable and MindWP will not use it; software platforms already anchor the price low; and MindWP has no operational case studies yet.

**Enquiry Path Partner.** Own the path from being found to being handled, with websites and specific problems as the doors. Chosen.

## Why Enquiry Path Partner

It answers the question the other two could not: why do these capabilities belong in one business?

Because the buyer experiences one problem. They do not experience "a website problem" and "a CRM problem". They experience patients choosing someone else.

It also keeps both routes fully sellable, and lets the business grow into more of the path without repositioning later.

## Why the public category stays plain

The public category is plain: websites and connected enquiry systems for clinics and expert-led businesses.

"Enquiry Path Partner" is not a phrase buyers search for, and naming a model in public invites explaining the model in public. That is what went wrong with the previous documents and website.

So plain wording normally leads. The model stays available where it genuinely adds clarity and can be explained in a line.

## Why Smart Website Systems remains

It names MindWP's website engagement and the standard behind it: a premium website designed for useful connection. Connected responsibilities are added when a business needs them, not by default.

That is why a website-only project is still Smart Website Systems, and why combined work needs no second name.

A separate "Premium Websites" offer was rejected. Buyers cannot tell two website offers apart or price the difference, so it would only cause confusion.

## Why website work stays commercially central

It is the recognised category, the strongest skill, the fastest revenue, and the most visible proof.

The market has split: cheap template and AI-built sites at the bottom, and a persistent specialist band above it. Undifferentiated website work is being squeezed. Website work built around a real customer decision is not.

Website builds and rebuilds therefore stay the main website proposition. Website improvement is a useful second option, not a replacement for it.

## Why connected work stays independently sellable

Many good clients already have a capable website. Forcing a rebuild would lose them for no reason.

Existing clients are also the most realistic route into connected work, because trust already exists.

## Why Local SEO is a major door

Being found comes before being contacted. For clinics, most of that happens through local search and reviews, and both carry real weight in whether a business is seen at all.

It is a substantial service, it sells on its own, and it has a natural recurring shape.

The earlier research flagged local SEO as a crowded and proof-hungry category. That is a delivery and marketing warning, not a reason to demote it. Real demand should decide how much attention each door gets.

The service was renamed from "Local SEO Authority" to plain "Local SEO". "Authority" is industry jargon, it reads as a boast, and it contradicts Foundation's own line that authority comes from evidence rather than adjectives. In a category where competitors shout about domination and rankings, a plain name is itself a signal.

## Why not every capability becomes a service

Capabilities and offers are different things.

A capability becomes a service only when it can be scoped, priced and delivered on its own. A service becomes a door only when a buyer would search for it by name.

If every capability got equal status there would be no hierarchy, the website would turn into a menu, and nothing would be clearly for sale.

Voice AI and broader automation are useful capabilities that are not ready to be public offers. Saying so honestly costs nothing.

## Assumptions

- Capacity is effectively one person, so scope discipline matters more than ambition.
- Published proof is currently thin.
- Premium website demand in the UK clinic market is reachable.
- Selling connected work to existing website clients is learnable as a process.
- Clinics are the strongest available starting market.

## Risks accepted

- The path model claims breadth MindWP has not yet demonstrated end to end.
- Doors bring in work that does not automatically prove the wider model.
- Recurring revenue sold before the delivery process is mature tends to churn early.
- AI tools keep eroding the perceived value of implementation skill.
- One person cannot market and deliver every capability at once.

## Evidence gaps

- Little UK-specific data on how clinic buyers behave; most available research is American.
- No recent reliable research on enquiry response times, despite how often old figures are quoted.
- MindWP's own numbers do not exist yet: how often website clients buy connected work, what a Review costs in time, what it converts at, and what UK clinics will pay.

## What would justify changing direction

- Premium website pricing proves unachievable despite good proof.
- Connected work converts far better than website work.
- Local SEO produces poor results or unhappy clients.
- A managed client churns before month six.
- The first full end-to-end client is documented — at which point the path model can move to the front of public language.
- The market focus changes.

## Superseded thinking

The previous documents treated the website as the centre of the business and connected work as adjacent to it. That framing is replaced: the path is the model, and the website is the strongest part of it.

They also argued their case at length inside the documents themselves, which is why internal reasoning kept appearing on public pages. The reasoning now lives here instead.
