# MindWP foundation

This document owns what does not change often: identity, audience principles, responsibility, truth, proof, privacy, professional boundaries, non-claims, and the durable role of the website.

It does not own offers, packaging, prices, campaigns, page plans or wording. Those belong to [STRATEGY.md](./STRATEGY.md) and [WRITING.md](./WRITING.md).

Durable means expected to last. An explicit later decision from the owner can still change it.

## What MindWP is

MindWP is an **Enquiry Path Partner** for clinics and expert-led businesses.

The enquiry path is how a business is found, considered, contacted, handled, followed up, and supported by reputation over time.

MindWP may work on any part of that path.

This is the internal organising model. It explains why website build and connected enquiry work belong in one business.

It is not the required public category, headline or website structure. It may be used publicly where it genuinely improves clarity and is explained in plain language. Strategy owns the public category.

The path describes responsibility MindWP may take. It does not claim completed work across every stage.

## Who MindWP serves

Clinics are the primary audience. Expert-led businesses are secondary.

An expert-led business is one where named expertise and professional judgement shape the service, and the buyer checks credibility and trust before making contact. Clinics, professional practices and advisory firms usually qualify. Businesses competing mainly on price, stock, venue or availability usually do not.

The buyer is the owner, or the person responsible for how the business is found and how enquiries are handled.

Fit depends on:

- the real decision the customer or patient is making;
- the value and trust at stake;
- the evidence available;
- the client's capacity to act;
- whether MindWP can own a useful scope.

There is no fixed rule about company age, size or location. Qualification helps judgement; it is not a filter that excludes anyone who fails one test.

## What MindWP is responsible for

MindWP is responsible for the mechanism: planning it, building it, connecting it, testing it, and keeping agreed parts working.

MindWP is not responsible for the client's commercial, clinical, legal or financial results.

Supporting growth and promising growth are different claims. Only the first is available.

## The website's durable role

The website is the part of the path MindWP controls most directly and a buyer can inspect most easily. It is where someone understands the work, judges whether to trust it, and decides to make contact. It is also MindWP's clearest proof of its own quality.

It is not the whole business. Handling what happens after contact is a real capability beside it, not an extra.

A rebuild is never a condition of working with MindWP. A business with a capable website is a valid client.

## Truth and proof

Authority comes from evidence, not adjectives.

- Never invent clients, testimonials, reviews, ratings, locations, dates, rankings, metrics, revenue, results or guarantees.
- Constructed or demonstration work must not be mistaken for client work. Its status must be clear. How that is shown is a page and design decision.
- Do not use recycled industry statistics. Much of what circulates in this market — missed-call figures, response-time claims, follow-up statistics — is old, based on tiny samples, or has no traceable source. If MindWP cannot stand behind a number, MindWP does not use it.
- Do not claim that every competitor fails.

Building proof is active work, not something to wait for. A thin proof surface is answered by getting better evidence, never by lowering the standard.

## Privacy and professional boundaries

- Keep enquiry data, client data, credentials, account details and private implementation detail out of public copy, examples, logs and source control.
- Use client and patient information only with proper authority and for the agreed purpose.
- MindWP speaks as "we". Founder and team details — name, photo, biography, credentials, years of experience, location — stay out of public copy unless the owner approves the exact use.
- "We" must not imply more people, a longer history, a studio or wider capability than exists. If a claim depends on headcount or tenure, leave it out.
- MindWP is not a clinical, legal, compliance or financial decision-maker.
- Automation never replaces professional judgement.
- Acknowledgement, routing, follow-up and review requests stay truthful, permission-aware and accountable to a named person.
- Follow the rules that apply to review requests and automated contact in the markets MindWP serves.

## Durable non-claims

MindWP does not:

- manage advertising;
- resell CRM software or any platform;
- present itself as a SaaS product, an automation package or an AI replacement;
- present a third-party platform as its own system;
- sell a compulsory bundle;
- promise rankings, leads, bookings, ratings, revenue, conversion improvement or professional outcomes without real evidence;
- take over the client's commercial or professional judgement.

Software and platforms support delivery. They are never the product.

What MindWP sells is planned and implemented work: websites, and the connected business systems around them.

## How to use this document

Read it when a question is about identity, fit, permission, responsibility or a claim.

Do not read it for offer detail, current priorities or wording.

If Foundation and Strategy appear to disagree, Foundation wins on identity, permission and boundaries; Strategy wins on what is sold and how. If they genuinely contradict each other, report it rather than choosing quietly.
