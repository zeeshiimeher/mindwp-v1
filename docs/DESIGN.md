# MindWP design

This document owns durable visual quality: how a MindWP page is composed, how it looks, and how it feels to move through.

It does not own business meaning, offers or claims ([FOUNDATION.md](./FOUNDATION.md), [STRATEGY.md](./STRATEGY.md)), wording ([WRITING.md](./WRITING.md)), site structure ([INFORMATION-ARCHITECTURE.md](./INFORMATION-ARCHITECTURE.md)) or implementation ([ENGINEERING.md](./ENGINEERING.md)).

**Stability:** durable foundation.

## What Design owns

- visual quality;
- page and section composition;
- hierarchy;
- scale;
- use of space;
- responsive experience;
- the intent behind interaction and motion.

## What Design does not own

- business strategy, offers and claims;
- what a page means and must cover;
- final wording;
- how many pages or sections exist;
- coding and technical implementation.

The approved page plan owns the required meaning. The task prompt may add page-specific direction, and it is more specific than this document.

## The standard

MindWP should feel modern, premium and carefully designed — interactive where that genuinely helps, and visually substantial enough to be remembered.

Design may draw from strong agency, SaaS, product and editorial websites, and from interactive digital experiences.

**Visual style does not decide MindWP's business category.** Content, offers and claims control what the site means. An agency-like, SaaS-like, product-like or editorial register is a design decision, not a strategic one.

Ambition is expected. A page that is only correct has not met the standard.

## Composition

**A section's job does not determine how it looks.** The plan says what a section must do. It does not say what that section is made of, how large it is, or how much of the page it occupies.

An eyebrow, a headline and a paragraph are one part of a section, never the whole of it. The rest is made from hierarchy, scale, space, the dominant material, the visual relationships between elements, and the pacing between one section and the next.

Two habits weaken a page:

- answering every section job with the same safe editorial structure, so that ten different jobs arrive as ten variations of one arrangement;
- giving every section equal visual weight, which makes a page read flat however good its individual parts are.

A page needs passages that dominate and passages that recede. How that is achieved belongs to the page in hand; there is no single required method, and this document does not prescribe one.

## Responsive experience

Narrow widths should feel intentionally designed, not compressed. Keep the meaning and the priorities, and compose them for the width — rather than taking the wide layout and removing its air.

## Interaction and motion

Interaction and motion should improve meaning, focus or the experience of moving through a page. Where they do none of those, leave them out.

Their mechanism, lifecycle and accessibility belong to [ENGINEERING.md](./ENGINEERING.md).

## Judgement

The approved page plan and the task prompt guide the work. Where they are silent, use strong design judgement rather than the safest available answer.

The absence of a rule here is not a restriction, and nothing in this document overrides a direct instruction from Zeeshan.
