# Dormant industry plans

**Status: dormant. Non-authoritative preservation material.**

These plans are **not selected for publication**, are **not current execution briefs**, and
**must be reconciled with current `docs/STRATEGY.md` before any reuse**. They are retained because
the underlying research is useful, not because the businesses are in lane.

They were moved out of `docs/PAGE-PLANS.md` on 2026-07-25 when the audience lane was settled as
clinic-first plus expert-led businesses, with the United Kingdom as the primary market. Under the
settled definition, an expert-led business is one where identifiable expertise and accountable
judgement materially shape the service. That excludes businesses whose primary value is inventory,
venue access, transport, equipment hire or undifferentiated transaction handling — which is why
each entry below was set aside.

Per `docs/README.md` precedence rule 7, archived material is a non-authoritative preservation
source. Extract from it only when reconciled with current authority.

Two notes for whoever revisits this:

- Several entries use US-register terminology (black-car operators, private-pay care advisers,
  tax-resolution). Any reuse in a UK-primary context needs the terminology reconciled first.
- The retained active plans carry the same issue in one place: **Tax-Resolution & Tax-Investigation
  Practices** should probably be renamed to UK register before that page is written.
