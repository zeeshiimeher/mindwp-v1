# MindWP design authority

This document owns MindWP's durable visual judgement: shared brand grammar, art direction, meaning-bearing composition, page continuity, responsive intent, purposeful interaction and motion, and the criteria for judging a render.

Business truth, offer strategy, page meaning, claims, repository structure, CSS architecture, capture commands, publication and page state belong to [FOUNDATION.md](./FOUNDATION.md), [STRATEGY.md](./STRATEGY.md), the supplied page plan, [WRITING.md](./WRITING.md) and [ENGINEERING.md](./ENGINEERING.md).

Only durable, cross-page principles belong here. A principle earns its place by holding across pages, not by having resolved one.

## The quality standard

MindWP's website should feel considered rather than assembled, while remaining clear, truthful, useful and appropriate to a professional service business.

- **Calm confidence:** priority is unmistakable, and the page trusts its material to carry weight.
- **Commercial seriousness:** the page connects customer reality, the offer, proof, boundaries and a useful next action.
- **Distinctive craft:** typography, spacing, hierarchy, media, edges, depth and responsive behaviour feel deliberate.
- **Trustworthiness:** visual material represents something real. It never simulates proof, software capability, certainty or operational detail that does not exist.
- **Clarity:** the main argument and important relationships can be understood before every paragraph is read.

Where an important composition reads as generic or interchangeable with unrelated material, strengthen the underlying relationship and hierarchy — that is what surface polish cannot supply.

## Sector-neutral visual grammar

MindWP's specialist depth is the precision of its thinking rather than any one industry's aesthetic. Independent clinics are the primary audience and may lead audience language, content and proof; the visual grammar stays sector-neutral.

The shared visual language — artefacts, illustration, iconography and recurring visual examples — holds across sectors, so the same brand can credibly serve a clinic, a law boutique or an advisory firm. Sector imagery, terminology and visual material belong to industry pages and other page-specific work, and join the shared visual system only by explicit adoption rather than by recurrence.

## Shared foundations

MindWP uses a recognisable visual family: navy, white, paper and mist surface roles; serif and sans-serif typographic character; eyebrow, heading and supporting-copy structures; centred, left-aligned and split section introductions; consistent typography and spacing roles; stable container and readable-measure discipline; semantic sections and familiar web-layout foundations.

**Familiar structures are used confidently when they fit the material.** Semantic sections, the shared section and container roles, the shared section-introduction structures, Flex, Grid, cards, lists, panels, tabs, timelines and grouped content are all valid starting points where the information genuinely has that shape. Section introductions may be left-aligned, centred, split or embedded according to the communication job; each alignment is equally professional.

**Recurring brand grammar creates coherence.** Repeated surfaces, typography roles, section-header grammar, containers, long pages and several horizontal bands are shared MindWP language, and a page reads as strong when it looks like itself. Distinctiveness comes from hierarchy, relationship, scale, craft and the experience created within those foundations.

**Meaning-specific inner composition creates distinction.** Repetition is judged by function and experience rather than by counting components: the question is whether a recurring pattern gives genuinely different meanings the same inner hierarchy, payload structure, scale and reading path, or whether it provides useful continuity between things that really are alike.

The `eyebrow → heading → supporting paragraph` opening is valid and may recur where it serves the material. It becomes limiting only when the whole section repeatedly resolves to `introduction → explanation → small visual object → next horizontal band`. The introduction is one part of the section design; the material after and around it carries the section's particular relationship.

Start from the existing shared foundations, and extend them where a composition has a requirement they do not serve. A page-specific visual layer may break out of a container, introduce controlled layering or establish a different spatial environment while the semantic structure and readable content remain sound.

### Typography character

MindWP's typography combines editorial character with operational clarity.

- **Fraunces** carries major ideas, emotional emphasis, important turns and editorial character.
- **Inter** carries explanation, evidence, navigation, labels, controls and operational detail.
- **Italics** signal meaningful contrast, qualification or a change in thought.
- **Readable measure** matters as much as font size. Explanatory copy stays at a width that keeps it easy to follow.

Inherit the established typographic roles first, and introduce a different scale, style, measure or behaviour where the composition requires a deliberate typographic role. Engineering owns how that inheritance is implemented.

### Surface and colour roles

- **Navy** carries conviction, control, connected-system logic, decisive moments and strong closure.
- **Paper** supports editorial explanation, argument and calm long-form clarity.
- **Mist** supports quieter evidence, reflection, qualification and transitions.
- **White** provides clarity, contrast and contained material within lighter environments.

These roles guide judgement, and a surface may support another job when the composition justifies it. A long page may reuse any of them several times.

Surface changes support progression, atmosphere, contrast, emphasis or a meaningful change in reading behaviour. Hierarchy, scale, density, relationship and focal emphasis remain the primary means of distinguishing one section from another; a background change works alongside them.

**Emerald marks controlled attention** — eyebrows, marks, rules and small emphasis; connection paths and meaningful system relationships; active, selected, success and status states; focus treatment; a primary action that deserves clear priority; and small operational fills inside truthful visual material. Its strength comes from that reservation.

### Edge, depth and material character

MindWP's material character favours hairline borders, restrained radii, subtle surface contrast, controlled shadows, intentional layering, clear spacing and proportion, and depth that communicates belonging, priority, state or interaction.

Overlap, rotation, layering and curvature suit relationship, belonging, sequence and depth. The useful test is whether removing the effect would cost the reader information.

Premium character comes primarily from typography, scale, proportion, spacing, hierarchy, material contrast and careful responsive behaviour. Restraint and presence work together: important material carries sufficient scale, contrast, depth, evidence or spatial presence to do its job, and a depth effect earns its place by being visible against its own background. Confirm that in the render rather than in the intent.

## Composition expresses the material's relationship

Three layers stay distinct: **shared grammar** provides the recognisable foundations, **composition** expresses the hierarchy and relationship specific to this material, and **technique** supplies the tools.

A technique is selected because it suits the meaning. Equal cards suit genuine peers, a timeline suits a sequence, a plain horizontal band suits a quiet transition. Reuse an inner pattern where the underlying relationship really is the same. Meaning determines what must be communicated; the component that communicates it stays a design choice.

For important or uncertain material, this progression usually helps — as a way to think, not a sequence to complete:

> section job → intended visitor change → the relationship that should become visible → focal hierarchy and eye path → relationship with neighbouring material → narrow-width transformation → purposeful motion or interaction

The visitor change should be concrete. After this material, should the visitor understand a distinction, trust a claim, compare alternatives, see causality, recognise ownership, follow a sequence, inspect proof, choose a path or feel ready to act?

The relationship might be comparison, sequence or causality, a whole and its parts, evidence and conclusion, context and focal point, choice and consequence, responsibility and handoff, or continuity and deliberate interruption. Choose the one that clarifies the material, and treat metaphors in copy as meaning cues rather than visual instructions.

Composition works through scale, span, contrast, negative space, alignment, sequence, containment, breakout, overlap, layering, density and transition. Changing one or two purposefully establishes hierarchy on its own. Additional ingredients earn their place by improving the central relationship.

Visual material can be many things — a constructed scene, real evidence or media, a model of relationships, language set at artefact scale, a spatial sequence, or well-set copy and rules alone. That list is illustrative and deliberately incomplete. Choose material that externalises the section's relationship, and judge it by whether that relationship is legible in the render, survives responsive transformation and stands without a paragraph explaining it.

Develop content and composition together, using credible real content early so the composition is shaped by what it actually has to carry. Proof, metrics, testimonials, systems, client results and operational detail are never invented to complete a visual idea.

## Weight, payload and quiet sections

A complete page varies scale, density, visual importance and reading path across its sections. Page-level judgement establishes changes in emphasis and atmosphere, shifts between dense and quiet material, differences in reading effort, meaningful continuity between connected ideas, stronger treatment for commercially important or proof-bearing material, and calm treatment where spectacle would add nothing.

**Quiet sections are valid and necessary.** Distinctiveness concentrates where the meaning benefits from it.

**Focal scale is earned by focal payload.** Before giving a section focal treatment, identify the dominant object and the relationship it reveals — that object is what the extra depth is holding. Height, padding and motion support emphasis that a payload already carries. These are proportions to judge rather than measurements to hit.

## Page continuity

A complete page makes its commercial progression, changes of argument, proof and intended action perceptible while distributing visual weight unevenly. Judge progression and changes in understanding, anticipation and payoff, continuity between related ideas, deliberate interruption when the argument changes, shifts in scale and reading effort, placement and legibility of proof, preparation for the primary action, and accumulated fatigue.

Necessary cautions read best when they are consolidated, allowed to recede into supporting copy, or placed beside the claim they qualify. Several boundaries each claiming focal scale flattens the progression they are meant to protect, and narrow widths amplify that — so check there too.

Narrative acts and surface rhythm are separate systems that inform each other. One narrative act may move through several surfaces, and one surface environment may carry meanings from more than one act; compose the surface rhythm across the complete page according to the communication need. Several sections may share one visual environment when they form a continuous experience, and each meaning inside that group keeps a clear role, hierarchy and transition of its own.

## Responsive recomposition

Major compositions need a credible narrow-width reading order while their concepts remain adaptable. Responsive design is recomposition.

Narrow widths preserve meaning and truthful boundaries, priority and focal hierarchy, the essential relationship the composition carries, proof and its connection to the relevant claim, the intended action, and essential interaction.

**Linear stacking suits linear meaning.** Comparison, causality, sequence, ownership and proof association need a narrow-width composition that keeps those relationships intact — achieved by reordering presentation, simplifying supporting decoration, changing crop, reducing layering or replacing a dense visual explanation with a clearer one, while the reading order stays coherent.

## Interaction and motion purpose

Interaction and motion serve orientation, state, causality, connection, meaningful emphasis and spatial continuity.

The affirmative policy:

- ordinary content is immediately readable;
- interaction provides a useful state or a real choice;
- authored motion concentrates where movement explains something;
- the static composition contains the complete meaning.

**Essential meaning is always available without hover, animation or a transient state**, and reduced-motion and script-free renders retain the same information. Interaction never simulates functionality the service does not provide.

Engineering owns the technical lifecycle, input states, accessibility, reduced-motion implementation and performance.

## Truthful visual material

Visual material represents something real: evidence, a truthful constructed explanation, an actual state or a clearly labelled demonstration. It should make meaning easier to perceive, evidence easier to inspect or an action easier to understand.

Constructed interfaces, dashboards or records may explain a real relationship when they are clearly identified as demonstrations. They must never imply client implementation, live functionality, measured results or operational reality that does not exist.

Real evidence works best large and legible enough to judge, cropped for the important content and kept connected to the claim it supports. Where a reasonable visitor could mistake constructed work for a client result, label it.

These boundaries are absolute:

- Constructed material must never masquerade as client evidence, a measured result or a live operational state.
- A demonstration must never be presented as a real implementation, and its status stays identifiable wherever a visitor could mistake it.
- Capability must never be implied beyond what the service actually provides.
- Private information, client data and operational detail must never appear in public visual material.

A useful check on any constructed scene is what it demonstrates. Where the honest answer is only that the work exists, the scene is decoration and the section is better served by material that shows a real relationship.

Mixed visual languages work well when each form has a distinct job and the page stays coherent.

## Judging rendered evidence

**Visual quality is established by fresh rendered evidence.** Render while content and composition can still change, and read the complete page before any section crop — page rhythm, accumulated fatigue and emphasis distribution live at page scale, and a section that reads well alone can still be the fourth of its kind. Thumbnails misrepresent type size and contrast in both directions, so confirm a suspected craft problem at full resolution before naming it.

Engineering owns the capture commands. `mindwp-design-eye` owns the review procedure. This document owns what to judge:

- **Inner composition:** setting aside palette, typography, surface, alignment and introduction grammar, does the important inner composition express this material's relationship?
- **Material:** does the relationship exist in the render, or only in the copy? Cover the paragraphs and see what remains.
- **Object craft:** is each payload legible against its own background, uncropped at its intended width, and understandable before the copy beside it?
- **Earned emphasis:** does each section given focal scale hold a payload that earns it?
- **Quiet by decision:** where a section is calm, does that read as a judgement about the material?
- **Meaning:** does the visual structure make a real comparison, sequence, dependency, proof relationship or decision easier to understand?
- **Hierarchy:** is the most important idea or evidence visibly unmistakable?
- **Recurrence:** where a reading pattern repeats, does the material genuinely share that role? Where meanings differ, do their hierarchy, scale and eye path differ for a reason?
- **Grouping:** does a shared environment create one continuous experience?

Use these as judgement prompts.
