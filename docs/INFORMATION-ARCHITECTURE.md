# MindWP information architecture

This document owns the current website structure: which pages exist or are planned, what each page is responsible for, which pages own the main commercial offers, and how navigation is grouped.

It does not own business meaning, offers, claims or public-content rules ([FOUNDATION.md](./FOUNDATION.md), [STRATEGY.md](./STRATEGY.md), [WRITING.md](./WRITING.md)), and it does not own visual style, layout, composition, interaction or motion. It answers the open decision recorded in Strategy section 13.

**Stability:** current approved direction. Authoritative now, and revisable by an explicit later decision.

## 1. Purpose

The IA exists so that page work starts from a settled structure instead of re-deciding it each time. It says what a page is for at the site level. It does not say what a page argues — that belongs to the page plan.

Three things follow from it:

- a new page has a defined role before anyone writes it;
- offers have one clear home, so the same argument is not written twice;
- the structure can grow without being rebuilt.

A page listed here is not a live route. Routes and publication come from source and an explicit release decision.

## 4. Main navigation direction

A clean main navigation around five items:

**Services · Work · Industries · About · Contact**

The Services area makes the four main commercial pages easy to reach: Smart Website Systems, Connected Enquiry Systems, Local SEO, Reputation & Reviews. Smart Website Systems, Connected Enquiry Systems and Local SEO are the three routes that carry the business commercially; Reputation & Reviews sits beside them as a service that can be bought on its own.

The Industries area groups the sector pages and links to the Industries hub.

Campaign pages normally stay outside the main navigation. They are reached through search, campaigns and relevant internal links.

This is direction, not a lock. Final navigation, grouping and labels may change during website planning and design.

## 5. Footer direction

Four groups:

- **Services** — the Services page and the four commercial pages
- **Industries** — the sector pages that exist
- **Company** — About, Work, How We Work, Contact
- **Legal** — Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, Terms, Accessibility

Campaign pages normally stay out of the footer as well, so that a campaign does not become a permanent fixture simply by being linked everywhere.

## 6. What each page is responsible for

| Page                      | Responsibility                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Home                      | Introduces MindWP and routes visitors. It does not explain every service fully                                                                                 |
| Services                  | The complete commercial overview — what MindWP does and how the routes fit together                                                                            |
| Smart Website Systems     | Owns the website engagement                                                                                                                                    |
| Connected Enquiry Systems | Owns CRM, enquiry capture, response, routing, ownership, follow-up and suitable automation at family level                                                     |
| Local SEO                 | Owns local discovery and visibility — being found and considered before an enquiry exists                                                                      |
| Reputation & Reviews      | Owns review requests, feedback and responsible replies                                                                                                         |
| Industries hub            | Shows which sectors MindWP works with and routes to the sector pages                                                                                           |
| Industry pages            | Apply the services to one sector's real situation                                                                                                              |
| Campaign pages            | Own one recognised problem, and route to the service that solves it                                                                                            |
| Work                      | Owns inspectable proof                                                                                                                                         |
| Project pages             | One piece of work, with its status clear                                                                                                                       |
| About                     | What MindWP is, who it serves, its point of view, and why its capabilities belong together. Within Foundation's limits on founder and team detail              |
| How We Work               | What the working relationship and a typical engagement generally feel like, at the level a buyer needs before making contact. Supporting; publication optional |
| Contact                   | Owns the normal enquiry action                                                                                                                                 |
| Enquiry confirmation      | Confirms the enquiry arrived and says what happens next                                                                                                        |
| 404                       | Returns a lost visitor to Services or Contact                                                                                                                  |
| Legal pages               | Required disclosure                                                                                                                                            |

**There is no separate Visibility & Enquiry Review page.** It is a conversation-led method that may follow contact, not something a buyer orders. The name may appear on Contact or the enquiry confirmation where it genuinely helps explain what happens next.

## 7. Offer ownership and adjacent mentions

Each commercial offer has exactly one owning page: Smart Website Systems, Connected Enquiry Systems, Local SEO, Reputation & Reviews.

**Pages that may mention an adjacent offer without owning it:**

| Page                      | May mention                                                       |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Home                      | All four, briefly, as routes                                      |
| Services                  | All four — this is the one page carrying the complete picture     |
| Smart Website Systems     | Connected enquiry work and Local SEO as things that may follow    |
| Connected Enquiry Systems | Reputation & Reviews, and website work where capture is the issue |
| Local SEO                 | Reputation & Reviews as a visibility factor, and website work     |
| Reputation & Reviews      | Local SEO and connected enquiry work                              |
| Industry pages            | Any service relevant to that sector                               |
| Campaign pages            | The one service that solves the named problem                     |

**Rule:** a page may link to a related service. It may not repeat that service's complete argument. The owning page makes the case; every other page names it and links.

## 8. Overlap rules

- **Lead Response & Handling and Follow-Up & CRM have no pages.** Their capabilities appear inside the Connected Enquiry Systems page. They remain useful service and scoping names in Strategy, proposals and conversations.
- **Reputation & Reviews and Local SEO.** Reputation & Reviews owns asking for reviews, feedback and replies. Local SEO owns being found and considered. Reviews may be named on the Local SEO page as a factor, with a link — the review argument is written once, on its owning page.
- **Website capture and connected handling.** The website engagement includes useful enquiry capture. Connected Enquiry Systems owns what happens to an enquiry after that, and capture from sources beyond the website.
- **Industry pages and service pages.** An industry page applies the services to a sector. It does not restate a service's full argument.
- **Campaign pages and service pages.** A campaign page owns a problem. The service page owns the offer.
- **Home and Services.** Home routes. Services carries the complete picture. Home does not become an explainer.

## 9. Current planned structure and possible future pages

**Current planned structure:** Home, Services, the four commercial pages, Industries hub and the sector pages listed, Work, About, Contact, enquiry confirmation, 404, and the four legal pages. How We Work sits here as a supporting page.

This is the shape the site is planned around. It is not a list approved for production, and listing a page does not put it in a queue. Each one is published when it is useful.

**Possible future pages:**

- **Voice AI.** Not a main service. It stays a developing capability inside the wider Connected Enquiry Systems work. It may later receive a focused campaign or capability page when MindWP is ready to market and support it. It does not belong in the main service navigation and is not equal to Smart Website Systems, Connected Enquiry Systems or Local SEO. A Missed Calls client may be offered Voice AI where it is suitable, but Voice AI is not the only solution to missed calls.
- **Separate Lead Response & Handling or Follow-Up & CRM pages.** Reconsider only if one of them develops a clearly separate buyer problem, real demand and enough distinct content.
- **Further sectors and professional categories.** Added one at a time.
- **Further campaign pages** for other clear, problem-led topics.
- **Location or city pages.** Not in the current structure. Reconsider only when there is real local demand, local proof, a meaningful market difference, or enough distinct content. The United Kingdom and Australia are markets, not automatic location-page templates.

**Industry pages must not be simple name-swapped copies.** Each should eventually own a distinct sector problem, customer or patient decision, enquiry pattern, useful capabilities or professional context. A sector page with nothing sector-specific to say is not ready to be published.

**Campaign pages do not become permanent services merely by existing.** They may be retired, changed or expanded later.

## 10. Publishing priorities

**Publish when useful.** What follows is guidance for sequencing, not a build order. Zeeshan decides what is built next.

| Group                  | Usually published when                                                                         |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Core and commercial    | The site first goes live — Home, Services, the four commercial pages, Contact, legal           |
| Supporting and utility | Alongside the core pages — About, enquiry confirmation, 404. How We Work whenever it is useful |
| Industry               | A sector page has something sector-specific to say                                             |
| Proof                  | There is real work to show, or clearly labelled demonstration work                             |
| Campaign               | A campaign is actually running                                                                 |
| Future                 | Its own condition is met — Voice AI, further sectors, further campaigns                        |

Nothing here fixes a date or requires one page before another. A page may be brought forward whenever there is a reason to.

## 11. Status of this document

**Planned does not mean built or published.** Listing a page here records its role in the structure. It does not commit MindWP to building it, publishing it, putting it in navigation, or including it in the sitemap or indexing. A planned page becomes live only through an explicit release decision.

**This IA is the current website structure, not a permanent restriction.** It may be improved as the business, proof, demand and services develop. Pages may be added, merged, split, retired or reclassified, and navigation may change during website planning and design.

Change it by an explicit later decision, and record the change here.
